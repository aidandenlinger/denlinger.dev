Hi!
I don't mean to alarm you, but you're on my [blog].

## Who?

I'm Aidan!
I learn, think about systems, solve problems,
dive down rabbit holes, dream, listen,
engage with art (movies, music, books, games (video, board, et al.)),
exist, and do everything else that I do.
Writing is a new creative outlet for me.

I'm a software engineer by trade.
See the [About] page for up-to-date information about me.

## What?

I'm not quite sure what this blog will be focused on!
When I'm passionate about something, I'll write about it.
Nothing is off-limits.
so we'll see what I'm actually inspired to write about.
I expect to mainly discuss developer tools and setups I enjoy,
projects I work on, art that influenced me,
and sharing life experiences.

## Where?

We regret to inform you that due to a mysterious [series of tubes],
any Internet-connected device capable of accessing arbitrary domains
can be exposed to the contents of these posts
via an attacker-controlled HTTP request to <https://denlinger.dev>.
Researchers have successfully replicated this behavior
in a sandboxed environment.
We are awaiting [CVE] ID assignment.

## When?

Irregularly and spontaneously!
I don't want to write for the sake of posting something,
or I'll lose the joy of doing it.

## Why?

If I'm passionate about something,
I'd like to remember it and note why it sparked something in me.
Doing it in public, even if no one is reading,
[successfully tricks my brain][social brain] into
making a reflection I'm happier with.

> By teaching, we learn.

(by [Seneca]…? Maybe?)

## How?

I'll write a post and post it 😝

More specifically, I don't want to be a perfectionist about what I write,
or I won't post anything.
I enjoy the [Learn in Public] and [Digital Garden] philosophies.
For me, that means that my posts will aim to be
a resource I wish I had while learning,
but never be a perfectly crafted, carved-in-stone final opinion.
I will always be thinking and learning,
and I want the freedom to upload a rough idea and slowly flesh it out,
or entirely change my mind!
To be communicate about this,
I intend on having some form of [epistemic status] on posts
to detail how fleshed out an idea is.
Post edits can be tracked at this blog's [source repo].

Thanks for stopping by!
I hope you found, or find, something you enjoyed.

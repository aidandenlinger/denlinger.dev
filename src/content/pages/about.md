Hi, I'm Aidan Denlinger!
I learn, think about systems, solve problems,
dive down rabbit holes, dream, listen,
engage with art (movies, music, books, games (video, board, et al.)),
exist, and do everything else that I do.

As a software engineer, I love designing and analyzing systems,
working with [DevOps] and creating [developer tools],
and solving challenging software problems for developers and customers.
I graduated from [UCSD] with a B.S. in Computer Science in 2022
and an M.S. in Computer Science in 2023.
Most recently, I worked at [Cubist] from 2024 - 2026,
shipping new features across a Rust backend, Rust CLI, and TypeScript SDK;
improving the reliability of our Rust test suite and GitHub Actions CI/CD;
and creating new monitoring for admin events.

I'm currently looking for my next problem to tackle.
If you have an opportunity and would like to connect,
please reach out on [LinkedIn]!
